PERMANENT LOADOUT

Roll gear. Build a set.

Keep the hunt for better rolls. Common items have one rolled stat; other rarities have two. Higher rarity improves the roll floor while stat caps stay fixed. This workbench lets you choose a set and intrinsic to compare builds; it is not the campaign's drop system.
