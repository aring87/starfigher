# Short sound prompts — Starfighter: Last Signal

The [original detailed prompts](AUDIO_PROMPTS_DETAILED.md) are also available.

For the field shown in ElevenLabs, use [exactly 500 characters per prompt](AUDIO_PROMPTS_500_CHARACTERS.md). The field counts characters, including spaces and punctuation, rather than words. The earlier 500-word pack is a reference brief and does not fit that input.

Standing preference: provide copy-ready ElevenLabs or Suno prompts with every sound pass. ElevenLabs prompts must fit the 500-character input; count characters, not words, including spaces and punctuation. Keep labels and mixing notes outside the prompt. The short variants below are under 100 characters. Keep the supplied intro and music-only boss fade. No boss alarm.

The supplied effects are now installed in v151 with synthesis fallback; see [Sound assets](SOUND_ASSETS.md). These prompts remain available for future variants. Durations are usable event targets; any longer generated tail can be trimmed.
