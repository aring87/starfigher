# ElevenLabs prompts — exactly 500 characters each

Use these for the **500-character** field shown in the screenshot. It counts characters, including spaces and punctuation, not words. Each plain text file below contains exactly 500 ASCII characters with no trailing newline. Headings are outside the prompt.

The earlier [500-word pack](AUDIO_PROMPTS_500_WORDS.md) is a reference brief and does not fit this field. [Short prompts](AUDIO_PROMPTS.md) remain available. The supplied effects are installed in v151 with synthesis fallback; see [Sound assets](SOUND_ASSETS.md). These prompts remain available for future variants.

## Hull hit

[Copy this prompt](artifacts/audio-prompts-500-characters/hull-hit.txt).

```text
One isolated starfighter hull impact, 0.35 seconds. Start with a sharp metallic crack, followed by a deep descending electronic thud and a tiny strained alloy resonance. Close, dry and tactile, with a fast attack, controlled bass and a short decay into silence. Communicate real damage while the ship remains intact. Heavier and rougher than a shield hit, clear under gunfire without harsh peaks. No music, voice, alarm, ambience, projectile travel, repeated hits, long reverb or full ship explosion.
```

## Shield hit

[Copy this prompt](artifacts/audio-prompts-500-characters/shield-hit.txt).

```text
One isolated energy shield absorbing a projectile, 0.25 seconds. Begin with a rounded electric impact, then a rising glassy shimmer that contracts into silence. Protective, resilient, and luminous, with a crisp yet soft onset and a short dry tail. Give it more body than a tiny defense block, while keeping it softer and brighter than metal hull damage. Close, centered and comfortable during repeated combat. No music, voice, alarm, ambience, glass shattering, explosions, extra hits or long reverb.
```

## Force / Bit block

[Copy this prompt](artifacts/audio-prompts-500-characters/force-block.txt).

```text
Create one tiny force-block sound: a crisp defensive tick followed immediately by a light, airy flick, lasting 0.05-0.09 seconds. Suggest a small energy shield catching a projectile at close range. Keep the transient soft, clean and precise, with a faint synthetic snap and a subtle breath of air. Quiet enough for frequent repeated blocks without fatigue. Start instantly and end with a dry, very short tail. No music, voice, alarm, ambience, ringing, heavy bass, large explosion or dramatic impact.
```

## Armor hit

Use a muted, rounded knock, a rough mineral texture and a quick friction rasp. Keep it quiet and compact, clearly confirming reduced damage without sounding like a full hit. Start immediately with a dry, very short tail. No music, voice, alarm, ambience, ringing, heavy bass, large explosion or dramatic impact.

## Core / weapon break

[Copy this prompt](artifacts/audio-prompts-500-characters/core-break.txt).

```text
One isolated alien weapon assembly breaking, 0.35 seconds. Start with a crisp metal and crystal snap, followed by a brief pressurized energy burst, a deep descending resonance and a rising energy glint. Tactile and controlled, with immediate onset and a quick dry decay to silence. This disables one attached weapon; the boss stays alive. Keep the fracture clear above gunfire without harsh peaks or booming bass. No music, voice, alarm, ambience, repeated explosions, debris sequence or long reverb.
```
