02 READ THE FIGHT

Collect the first colored crystal to equip your Force Pod. It blocks pink shots; amber fire pierces Force and Bits. Watch for amber charge cues and move before they fire. Red fires directly, blue ricochets, yellow seeks targets. Mission limits unlock stronger pickups as you progress.

Story weapon limits: Force Level 2 unlocks in mission 2 and Level 3 in mission 4. A second missile launcher and Defense Bit unlock in mission 3. Shields hold up to four charges. Surplus pickups add DOSE; crystals still change color.

Boss armor takes 25% damage. Watch the embedded cores: opening tells precede bright exposure windows. Break every core. Absorbing fire with the Force and destroying enemies fill DOSE.
